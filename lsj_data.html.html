Get the Data

Tutorial

Pull data from the Water Quality Portal

The Water Quality Portal (WQP) is the national clearinghouse for water quality monitoring data from USGS, EPA, and hundreds of state, local, and tribal agencies — including much of the raw monitoring data behind this plan. Use the steps alongside to run your own query.

Open the Water Quality Portal
  1. Set a location
    Search by state/county, a HUC8 watershed code, or a point + radius to narrow results to the Lower San Juan.
  2. Choose your sampling parameters
    Pick a Sample Media (e.g. Water), a Characteristic Group (e.g. Nutrients, Metals, Sediment), and a date range.
  3. Preview results
    The portal shows a count of matching sites and results before you download anything, so you can adjust your filters first.
  4. Download
    Choose Site data or Sample results (download both separately if you need site metadata too), then pick CSV or Excel.
