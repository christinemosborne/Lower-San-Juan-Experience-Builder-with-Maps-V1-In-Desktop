Mark places on the map

Drop a pin on any place in the watershed you'd like to tell us about — a concern, a project idea, or a place that matters to you. Add as many pins as you like, then continue to the short survey.

How to use the map

Click or tap the map to drop a numbered pin. Add as many as you like, and they don’t need to be exact. Drag a pin to move it, or click it (or the × in the list) to remove it. Search for a town or landmark to jump there. The box in the top right corner switches to aerial imagery and shows layers like Navajo Nation chapters. When you’re done, select Continue to survey. Your pins carry over, so you can refer to them by number (“Pin 2 is where the bank is eroding”).
